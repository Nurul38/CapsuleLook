# Privacy Policy for Visibee

*Last updated: January 2025*

## Introduction

Visibee ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application Visibee (the "Service").

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide
- **Photos**: Only clothing images you explicitly select and upload
- **Clothing Data**: Item names, brands, colors, categories, and tags you enter
- **Preferences**: Function categories, style preferences, and app settings
- **Optional Information**: Purchase links, notes, and custom tags

### Information We Do NOT Collect
- ❌ Your photo library contents (we don't browse your photos)
- ❌ Personal identifying information
- ❌ Location data
- ❌ Contact information
- ❌ Social media connections
- ❌ Browsing history outside the app

### Automatically Collected Information
- **Usage Analytics**: Anonymous app usage patterns to improve the service
- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system version (anonymous)
- **Error Reports**: Crash logs and performance data (anonymous)

## How We Use Your Information

### Primary Uses
- **Wardrobe Management**: Organize and display your clothing items
- **AI Analysis**: Process your photos to identify clothing types, colors, and styles
- **Personalization**: Provide style recommendations based on your preferences
- **App Improvement**: Enhance features and fix issues

### AI Processing
- Photos are processed using Google Gemini AI for clothing recognition
- Analysis happens securely and your photos are not shared with third parties
- AI results help with auto-tagging and style suggestions

## Data Storage and Security

### Security Measures
- All data is encrypted in transit and at rest
- Secure cloud storage with industry-standard protections
- Regular security audits and updates
- No third-party access to your personal wardrobe data

### Data Location
- Data is stored on secure cloud servers
- Backup systems ensure data preservation
- No data is stored on unsecured servers

## Your Rights and Control

### Data Control
- **Access**: View all your data within the app
- **Edit**: Modify or update any information at any time
- **Delete**: Remove individual items or your entire wardrobe
- **Export**: Download your data in a standard format

### Account Management
- You can delete your account and all associated data at any time
- Data deletion is permanent and cannot be recovered
- No account required for basic app functionality

## Third-Party Services

### AI Services
- **Google Gemini**: Used for image analysis (privacy-focused processing)
- Images are processed but not permanently stored by third parties

### Analytics (Anonymous)
- Basic usage analytics to improve app performance
- No personally identifiable information is included
- You can opt-out of analytics in app settings

## Children's Privacy

Visibee is safe for users 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information, please contact us immediately.

## International Users

This app is designed to comply with:
- **GDPR** (European Union)
- **CCPA** (California, USA)  
- **PIPEDA** (Canada)
- **Privacy Act** (Australia)

## Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify users of significant changes through:
- In-app notifications
- Email (if provided)
- App Store update notes

Continued use after changes indicates acceptance of the updated policy.

### App Description (4000 characters max)
Transform your wardrobe chaos into organized bliss with Visibee, the AI-powered clothing management app designed specifically for teenagers and women with ADHD.

**🌟 OVERCOME OBJECT PERMANENCE**
Never forget what you own again! Visibee helps you catalog every piece in your wardrobe with smart photo organization and AI-powered tagging.

**🤖 AI-POWERED FEATURES**
• Smart clothing recognition and auto-tagging
• Color detection and style analysis  
• Personalized Kibbe body type recommendations
• Korean & Western color analysis
• Face shape analysis for hijab styling

**👗 SMART ORGANIZATION**
• Function-based folders (Casual, Formal, Athletic, etc.)
• Seasonal categories (Winter, Summer, Spring, Autumn)
• Seamless item management between folders
• Visual 4×5 grid previews for quick browsing

**🧕 INCLUSIVE HIJAB STYLING**
• Worldwide hijab styles with step-by-step tutorials
• Malaysian hijabi-inspired designs
• Face shape compatibility matching
• Color coordination recommendations

**✨ ADHD-FRIENDLY DESIGN**
• Clean, distraction-free interface
• Optional fields - add as much or little detail as you want
• Visual organization with color-coded categories
• Quick access to frequently used items

**🔒 PRIVACY-FOCUSED**
• Only accesses photos you explicitly select
• No browsing of your photo library
• Secure cloud storage for your wardrobe data

**💡 KEY FEATURES**
• Photo inventory with AI analysis
• Smart auto-naming and indexing
• Brand and color management
• Purchase link tracking
• Tag-based organization
• Bulk selection and moving
• Offline capabilities

Perfect for students, young professionals, and anyone who wants to maximize their wardrobe potential while managing ADHD challenges.

Download Visibee today and discover the joy of an organized, accessible wardrobe!
